編者註 (Editor’s Note):
每頁左為原稿掃描，右為逐頁釋文與英文。釋文由機器視覺結合人工校讀而成；原稿為行草手寫、年代久遠，部分頁面褪色嚴重，括號 〔 〕 內為字跡難辨之推測或缺失標記。
英文為輔助閱讀之詮釋，非權威翻譯：散文頁（序文、源流、遷徙記）為全文翻譯，名錄頁僅附簡短摘要。家人於校讀器更正某頁中文後，該頁英文亦應隨之重新生成；在此之前，英文係依原始機器釋文，可能與已更正之內容不符。歡迎家人對照原稿協助校正。
Each page shows the original scan on the left and the page-by-page transcription + English on the right. English is an interpretive reading aid, not an authoritative translation — prose pages are translated in full; register / name-list pages carry a short summary only. When a page’s Chinese is corrected in the proofreader, its English should be regenerated.

English summaryA detailed, clearly written register of the modern Sabah branch. It records Peng Weizhao (彭慰照), apparently a daughter-in-law-to-be / foster bride, born in Sandakan (山打根) in 1946 and given up in adoption (過繼) at age one; a daughter Qingzhu (慶珠); references to Papar (吧巴); a birth on 15 October (lunar), December 1936; a third son Han- (漢), a second daughter Sumei (素枚柏?), an eldest daughter Sufen (素芬), and an eldest son Hankang (漢康) with his wife Su Ruilan (蘇瑞蘭); and Qi'an (其安), born in the ninth lunar month of Republic Year 8. It notes a marriage at the consulate in Sandakan on the 20th of the tenth lunar month, 1935 (Republic Year 24), solemnized by consul Mr. Wu Qinxun (吳勤訓). Some names remain unverified.
